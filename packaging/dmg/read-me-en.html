Install LanFi

Drag LanFi.app into the Applications folder next to this window, then eject the disk image and start LanFi from Applications.

This build is not signed or notarized

LanFi is distributed without an Apple Developer signature, so macOS may say “LanFi is damaged”, “cannot be opened” or “cannot verify the developer”. The app is fine — Gatekeeper only refuses software it cannot check. Choose one of these ways to open it:

Option 1 — right-click Open (recommended)

  1. Open Applications in Finder, then Control-click (or right-click) LanFi.
  2. Choose Open, then Open again in the dialog.
  3. Double-clicking works normally afterwards.

Option 2 — System Settings

  1. Try to open LanFi once and close the warning.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. In the “Security” section click Open Anyway and confirm.

Option 3 — Terminal, if macOS says “damaged”

Removing the quarantine flag is what the two steps above do through the UI:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LanFi.app

Requirements

macOS 13 Ventura or newer, Intel or Apple silicon (one universal build). LanFi asks for Location Services the first time because macOS reveals Wi-Fi names and BSSIDs through that permission; the scans themselves happen locally.

Checksums and the CLI archive are published with each release on GitHub. No account is needed and there is nothing to activate.
